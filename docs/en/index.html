(02) Studio

Studio

Jungle Pixel Studio is an independent video game studio born in Colombia.

We are small on purpose: every mechanic, every character and every button goes through our hands. We make the games we would rather be playing ourselves, the kind you tell your friends about the next day.

  • 01

    Gameplay first

    If it isn't fun with grey boxes, graphics won't fix it. We test early and we test a lot.

  • 02

    Handcrafted

    Code, art and sound made with a craftsperson's care. No shortcuts you can feel while playing.

  • 03

    Better together

    We love games you enjoy with friends, and the laughs when everything goes wrong.

(03) How we work

Process

  1. 01

    Idea

    It all starts with a silly question: “what if…?”. If it makes us laugh, it goes in the notebook.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    Grey boxes and half-baked physics. If it's already fun like that, we are on the right track.

  3. 03

    Playtests

    We play it with friends again and again. Whatever isn't fun gets cut, no regrets.

  4. 04

    Launch

    We polish, listen to the community and keep improving the game after release.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

In short: this website has no forms, uses no cookies, has no analytics or advertising and asks you for no data. We only handle the data you choose to send us by email, and only to reply to you.

This policy explains how Jungle Pixel Studio handles personal data, in accordance with Colombian Statutory Law 1581 of 2012, Decree 1377 of 2013 (now part of Single Regulatory Decree 1074 of 2015) and other Colombian personal data protection (habeas data) regulations.

1. Data controller

Trade name
Jungle Pixel Studio
Nature
Independent developer (natural person) publishing under the trade name Jungle Pixel Studio
Location
Colombia
Email
junglepixel2024 [at] gmail.com
Website
junglepixelstudio.github.io

2. What data we handle

Data you send us by email

If you write to us, we handle your email address, your name if you include it, the content of your message and any data or files you choose to attach. Please do not send us sensitive data (health, beliefs, biometric data, etc.) or third parties' data without their permission.

Hosting technical data

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (United States). Like any web server, GitHub logs the IP address of visitors for security purposes and to operate the service. We have no access to those logs and do not use them to identify you. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

What we do not do

  • We do not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, browser fingerprinting or advertising.
  • We do not embed third-party content (videos, maps, social buttons) that could track you.
  • We do not store anything in your browser's local storage.
  • We do not build profiles, make automated decisions about you, or sell, rent or share data.

3. What we use data for

  • Replying to your messages and enquiries: press, collaborations, support, business proposals or anything else.
  • Handling security reports and notices of possible rights infringements.
  • Responding to requests to exercise your data rights.
  • Complying with legal obligations and defending against possible claims.

We will not send you newsletters or advertising unless you expressly ask us to.

4. Authorization

By writing to us voluntarily you authorize us, through unequivocal conduct, to handle the data included in your message for the purposes of this policy. You may revoke that authorization at any time, except where we must keep the data because of a legal or contractual obligation.

5. Service providers and international transfers

To operate we use providers that process data on our behalf and whose servers may be located outside Colombia:

  • Google LLC (Gmail), to receive and answer emails.
  • GitHub, Inc., to host the site.

These providers apply internationally recognized security standards. Data is transferred to countries that the Colombian Superintendence of Industry and Commerce considers to provide an adequate level of protection or, otherwise, on the basis of your authorization, in accordance with article 26 of Law 1581 of 2012. We will only share data with authorities when required by law or court order.

6. How long we keep data

We keep emails for as long as needed to handle your request and, afterwards, for as long as liabilities may arise or the law requires. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

7. Your rights

As a data subject, article 8 of Law 1581 of 2012 gives you the right to:

  • access, update and correct your personal data;
  • request proof of the authorization you gave us;
  • be informed, upon request, of how we have used your data;
  • revoke your authorization and request deletion of your data when legal principles, rights and guarantees are not respected;
  • access your data free of charge;
  • file complaints with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce (sic.gov.co) once you have completed the enquiry or claim process with us.

8. How to exercise your rights

Email us at junglepixel2024 [at] gmail.com with the subject “Personal data”, stating your name, what you are requesting and where you want to receive the answer. To protect your data we may ask you to verify your identity.

  • Enquiries: answered within ten (10) business days, extendable by five (5) more business days if needed, in which case we will tell you why (article 14, Law 1581 of 2012).
  • Claims (correction, update, deletion or revocation): handled within fifteen (15) business days, extendable by eight (8) more business days, in which case we will tell you why (article 15, Law 1581 of 2012).

9. Visiting from other countries

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another place with its own data protection rules, you also have the rights those rules grant (for example, under the GDPR: access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability, as well as the right to complain to your supervisory authority). Our legal basis is your consent when you write to us and our legitimate interest in replying to the correspondence we receive.

10. Children

We do not knowingly collect data from children or adolescents. If you are a minor, please ask your parents or legal guardians to contact us. If we discover that we have received a minor's data without proper authorization, we will delete it.

11. Security

The site is static: it has no databases, forms or user accounts that could leak. It is always served over HTTPS, enforces a content security policy that only allows our own code and loads no third-party resources. We protect the accounts that manage the site and our email with strong passwords and two-factor authentication, and only the owner has access to our correspondence.

12. Changes and validity

This policy is effective as of November 4, 2026. If we change it in any meaningful way, we will say so on this page with the new date. Data will be processed for as long as the studio is active and it is necessary for the purposes described.

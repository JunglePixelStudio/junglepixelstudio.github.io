Last updated: November 4, 2026

3. What you can do without asking

  • Share links to this site and to our games' pages.
  • Quote short excerpts of our texts, citing the source.
  • Use screenshots, logos and press material published by us for news, reviews, analysis and articles about our games, without altering them.
  • Record videos and stream our games, including monetized content, under our content creator policy.
  • Create non-commercial fan art and other fan works, stating that the game belongs to Jungle Pixel Studio.

4. What is not allowed

  • Reproducing, distributing, modifying, selling, renting or commercially exploiting our content without written permission.
  • Extracting game assets (models, textures, sounds, music, code) to redistribute them or use them in other projects.

  • Registering or using names, domains, profiles or trademarks identical or similar to ours.
  • Using our content to train, fine-tune or feed artificial intelligence systems. We expressly reserve this use, including text and data mining.
  • Removing or altering copyright or authorship notices.

The terms of use of each game (end-user license, rules on mods or modifications) are stated in the game itself or in the store where it is distributed.

6. How to report an infringement

If you believe any content on this site infringes your rights, or if you see our content used without permission elsewhere, email us at junglepixel2024 [at] gmail.com with the subject “Copyright” and include:

  1. your name and contact details;
  2. the work or right you believe has been infringed;
  3. the exact address (URL) of the content in question;
  4. a statement that you are acting in good faith, that the information is accurate, and that you own the right or are authorized to act on the owner's behalf;
  5. your signature (your typed full name is fine).

We will review the request promptly and, where appropriate, remove or modify the content.

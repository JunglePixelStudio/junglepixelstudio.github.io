Content creator policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

In short: yes, you can make videos, streams, guides and reviews of our games and earn money from them. You don't need to ask us. We love seeing them!

1. What we allow

Jungle Pixel Studio gives you permission to create and publish content using footage and audio from our games —let's plays, streams, guides, reviews, analysis, clips, compilations, screenshots and thumbnails— and to monetize it through partner programs on platforms such as YouTube, Twitch, Kick or TikTok, donations, subscriptions or sponsorships of your channel.

You don't need to contact us first, although we'd love it if you let us know so we can watch it.

2. Conditions

  • Mention the game's name and, if you can, link to its store page or to this site.
  • Your content should add something of your own: your gameplay, your commentary, your editing. Re-uploading our official trailers or videos as they are is not allowed.
  • Do not imply that you are an official partner or that the studio sponsors or approves your content, unless we have agreed to it in writing.
  • Do not distribute the game, its files, assets or keys, or sell our content as a standalone product (for example, the soundtrack or art collections).
  • Follow the rules of the platform where you publish and the law.
  • Do not use our games in content that promotes hatred, discrimination, harassment or illegal activities.

3. Music

The original music of our games may play in your videos and streams while you play. If a platform sends you a claim about it, write to us and we'll help you sort it out. If any track in a game belongs to a third party with different terms, we will say so on that game's page.

4. Keys for press and creators

If you'd like a key to review or stream one of our games, email us at junglepixel2024 [at] gmail.com with a link to your outlet or channel and an idea of your audience. We can't guarantee keys for every request, but we read every message.

5. Commercial agreements

This policy does not cover paid campaigns, studio sponsorships, use of our content in third-party advertising or merchandise. For any of those, write to us and let's talk.

6. Scope of this permission

This permission is free, non-exclusive, worldwide and non-transferable. We may update this policy or ask you to remove a specific piece of content if it breaks these conditions. It does not affect any rights you have by law, such as the right to quote.

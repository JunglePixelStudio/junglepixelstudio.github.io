In short: this website uses no cookies and no trackers. That is why you don't see a cookie banner: there is nothing to accept.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that some websites store in your browser to remember information about you or your visit: sessions, preferences, statistics or advertising. There are similar techniques, such as the browser's local storage, tracking pixels or device fingerprinting.

2. What this website uses

Nothing. This site:

  • sets no first-party or third-party cookies;
  • does not use your browser's local or session storage;
  • has no analytics, advertising or embedded social media tools;
  • uses no tracking pixels or device fingerprinting.

Switching languages works through links to different pages, so no preference needs to be stored on your device. The visual effects run in your browser and send no information anywhere.

3. Sites we link to

If you follow a link to another website (for example, a game store or a social network), that website may use its own cookies under its own policy. We recommend checking it when you visit.

4. Managing cookies in your browser

Although we don't use them here, you can block or delete cookies from any website in your browser's privacy settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge or others).

5. If this changes

If we ever add analytics, embedded videos or any other tool that uses non-essential cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before enabling them.

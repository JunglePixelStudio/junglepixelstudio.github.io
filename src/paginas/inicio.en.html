{{icono:♣}} How we work

The trail of every game

  1. 1

    Idea

    It all starts with a silly question: “what if…?”. If it makes us laugh, it goes in the notebook.

  2. 2

    Prototype

    Grey boxes and half-baked physics. If it's already fun like that, we are on the right track.

  3. 3

    Playtests

    We play it with friends again and again. Whatever isn't fun gets cut, no regrets.

  4. 4

    Launch

    We polish, listen to the community and keep improving the game after release.

Jungle Pixel Studio

We are a small studio on purpose: every mechanic, every character and every button goes through our hands. We make the games we would rather be playing ourselves, the kind you tell your friends about the next day.

We love silly ideas that work: vacuuming a dragon's hoard, giving a peacock his tail back or opening the door at 3 AM. If something makes us laugh in the prototype, it moves forward.

The trail of every game

  1. 1

    Idea

    It all starts with a silly question: “what if…?”. If it makes us laugh, it goes in the notebook.

  2. 2

    Prototype

    Grey boxes and half-baked physics. If it's already fun like that, we are on the right track.

  3. 3

    Playtests

    We play it with friends again and again. Whatever isn't fun gets cut, no regrets.

  4. 4

    Launch

    We polish, listen to the community and keep improving the game after release.

In short: if you find a security issue on this website, email us at {{correo}} with the subject “Security”. We'll reply, fix it and, if you wish, give you credit.

1. How we protect this website

  • Static site: there is no server of our own, database, forms or user accounts to attack or leak.
  • Always HTTPS: every page is served encrypted.
  • Content Security Policy (CSP): the browser only runs code from this same site and blocks any external script, style or resource.
  • No third parties: we load no libraries, fonts, analytics or advertising from other servers.
  • Frame protection: the site refuses to be displayed inside other people's pages, to prevent clickjacking tricks.
  • Protected email: our address is shown in a way that makes it harder for spam bots to harvest.
  • Protected accounts: the accounts that manage the site, the code and our email use two-factor authentication.
  • Standard contact: we publish a security.txt file following RFC 9116.

2. How to report a vulnerability

Email us at {{correo}} with the subject “Security” and include, if possible:

  • a description of the issue and its potential impact;
  • the affected address (URL);
  • steps to reproduce it, screenshots or a proof of concept;
  • how you would like to be credited, or whether you prefer to remain anonymous.

3. Our commitments

  • We will acknowledge your report within five (5) business days.
  • We will keep you informed while we investigate and fix it.
  • If you wish, we will credit you publicly once the issue is resolved.
  • We will not take legal action against anyone who researches in good faith and follows the rules of this policy.

4. Research rules

  • Do not access, modify or delete data that isn't yours, and stop as soon as you have demonstrated the issue.
  • No denial-of-service attacks, spam, social engineering, phishing or physical attacks.
  • Do not run aggressive automated scanners.
  • Give us a reasonable amount of time (90 days) to fix the issue before disclosing it.

5. Scope

This policy covers the site {{dominio}}. Issues in third-party services (GitHub, Google, Steam or others) should be reported to those services through their own programs. If you find a security issue in one of our games, you can also email us at the same address.

We don't have a paid bug bounty program for now, but we truly appreciate every report.
